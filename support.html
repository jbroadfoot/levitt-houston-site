What your gift builds

A gift to Levitt Pavilion Houston is a gift the whole city keeps: free admission for everyone, every concert, every season, on an open lawn beside Willow Waterhole Greenway.

Your gift builds the stage, the lawn, and everything it takes to present 40 to 50 free concerts a year, and it joins national and public partners already invested in the project.

Naming opportunities

The pavilion will offer a range of future naming opportunities, from signature elements of the site to the stage itself. These are conversations we would love to have.

For a family, a foundation, or a leader of the industry that shaped this ground, this is a rare chance to put a name on a permanent civic landmark that welcomes everyone, free, for generations.
