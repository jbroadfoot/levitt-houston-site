The Levitt Network

A proven model,
coast to coast

From a former town dump in Westport to the blast furnaces of Bethlehem, Levitt venues turn overlooked places into beloved ones.

A national movement

Houston joins a proven network

The Mortimer and Mimi Levitt Foundation has spent decades turning underused public spaces into thriving destinations through one simple idea: free, live music brings communities together. Each year the Levitt network presents more than 1,000 free concerts in over 100 towns and cities.

Venues like ours

Transformation is the Levitt signature

SteelStacks, Bethlehem PA

Built at the base of retired Bethlehem Steel blast furnaces, Levitt Pavilion SteelStacks turned an industrial ruin into the heart of a celebrated arts campus. The closest cousin to Houston's derrick story.

Denver, Colorado

Levitt Pavilion Denver brings 50 free concerts a year to Ruby Hill Park, drawing thousands to a once-quiet corner of the city and proving the model at metro scale.

Sioux Falls, South Dakota

Levitt at the Falls opened in 2019 after a community capital campaign much like ours, and quickly became the city's shared front porch. Our playbook, already run.

The partnership

What the Levitt Foundation brings

Levitt venues are true partnerships. The Foundation has pledged a $500,000 capital grant toward construction, $1.1 million in operating support across the first five years, and a minimum of $200,000 annually through 2041. Just as valuable, Houston gains the network itself: national standards, shared learning across 100+ communities, and a brand audiences trust.

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston, our local nonprofit, programs the venue, produces the concerts, and leads the capital campaign, the same model that powers every Levitt venue in America.
