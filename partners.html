Bringing Levitt Houston
to life

Levitt Pavilion Houston is moving forward through a partnership among the Levitt Foundation, the Southwest Redevelopment Authority, the City of Houston, and Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston. Each brings a distinct role to creating a permanent home for free live music in Southwest Houston.

The partnership

Four roles, one project

Levitt Foundation

Provides the national Levitt model, experience, and ongoing support for free outdoor music.

Southwest Redevelopment Authority

Serves as the public property and site-development partner advancing the future venue location.

City of Houston

Serves as a public partner helping advance the future pavilion and broader community opportunity.

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston

The local nonprofit responsible for fundraising, programming, and operating Levitt Pavilion Houston.

Our roots

Rooted beside Willow Waterhole

Levitt Houston grew from years of free MusicFest concerts at Willow Waterhole Greenway. Today, the future pavilion is planned beside the 291-acre Greenway, creating a distinct Levitt destination with deep community roots.

Willow Waterhole Greenway.

Foundations and major donors

The public framework is in place and the Levitt Foundation is committed. What remains is the pavilion itself, and that is where Houston's philanthropic community writes its name into this story. Every private gift joins committed public and Foundation investment.
