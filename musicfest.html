MusicFest

Free music,
already happening

Our free festival at Willow Waterhole Greenway shows how Houston turns out for free live music.

Our signature event

A festival with a mission

Photo: MusicFest Spring 2026 crowd and lawn at Willow Waterhole

MusicFest has brought free live music to Willow Waterhole since 2013, featuring student musicians, professional artists, food, and a community gathered around the stage. The Spring 2026 event continued that tradition and showed the enthusiasm for live music that Levitt Houston will build upon.

Planning is underway for future Levitt concerts and MusicFest 2027. Join our email list for announcements.
