Milestones

The road to opening night

  • 2000s – Present Neighbors transform 291 acres of flood-control land into Willow Waterhole Greenway, building the trails, lakes, and community that make this site possible.
  • 2024 – 2025 The Levitt Foundation selects Willow Waterhole as the home of its Houston venue. Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston incorporates and begins producing free music at the greenway, including MusicFest.
  • June 2026 The Southwest Houston Redevelopment Authority, the Levitt Foundation, and Levitt Houston sign a Memorandum of Understanding: a 17-acre site acquisition, a 30-year $1-per-year lease, and more than $1.6 million in Foundation commitments.
  • 2026 Master planning for the Gasmer site begins under TIRZ 20. The capital campaign for pavilion construction launches.
  • Ahead Definitive agreements, pavilion design, groundbreaking, and a first season of 40 to 50 free concerts. Every milestone will be posted here.
