Milestones

The road to opening night

  • 2000s – Present Neighbors transform 291 acres of flood-control land into Willow Waterhole Greenway, building the trails, lakes, and community that make this site possible.
  • 2024 – 2025 The Levitt Foundation selects Willow Waterhole as the home of its Houston venue, and Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston incorporates.
  • Spring 2026 MusicFest fills Willow Waterhole Greenway with free live music, families, and neighbors from across Southwest Houston.
  • June 2026 The Southwest Houston Redevelopment Authority, the Levitt Foundation, and Levitt Houston formalize their partnership to develop the pavilion. Planning for the former Shell site continues.
  • Ahead Anticipated next steps include pavilion design, groundbreaking, and a first season of free concerts. Milestones will be posted here as they happen.

Levitt Pavilion Houston

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston is the Texas nonprofit building and programming Levitt Pavilion Houston, a free outdoor music venue rising beside Willow Waterhole Greenway in Southwest Houston.
