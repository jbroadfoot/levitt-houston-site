About Us

Built by the
community it serves

A volunteer-led Texas nonprofit with 25 years of roots at Willow Waterhole and one mission: free live music for everyone.

Who we are

Neighbors first

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston is a Texas nonprofit corporation formed to build, program, and sustain the Levitt Pavilion at Willow Waterhole. Our leadership comes from the same community that spent 25 years transforming the greenway itself.

We exist for one purpose: free, professional live music for everyone in Houston, presented 40 to 50 times a year in an open lawn setting, to the standards of the national Levitt network.

“Music connects us. Music uplifts us. Music creates community.” Levitt Pavilion Houston

Our roots

Born at Willow Waterhole

The pavilion grows directly out of the Willow Waterhole story. What began as flood-control land became, through 25 years of volunteer work, a 291-acre greenway of lakes, trails, prairie, and community events. When the Levitt Foundation went looking for its Houston home, this community's track record is what it found.

The Willow Waterhole Greenspace Conservancy remains a close partner, and MusicFest at the greenway carries both organizations' missions forward together.
