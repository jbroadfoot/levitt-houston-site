The campaign

What your gift builds

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston is leading the capital campaign to construct the pavilion. The Levitt Foundation's capital grant and long-term operating pledge, the Authority's land and lease, and Brays Oaks maintenance support are secured. Private philanthropy completes the picture.

Your gift builds the stage, the lawn, and the infrastructure for 40 to 50 free concerts a year, and it does so on land the community controls for the next 30 years.
