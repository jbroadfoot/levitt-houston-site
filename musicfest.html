MusicFest

Free music,
already happening

Our annual festival at Willow Waterhole Greenway previews what 40 to 50 free concert nights a year will feel like.

Our signature event

A festival with a mission

MusicFest is Levitt Pavilion Houston's annual free festival at Willow Waterhole Greenway: a full day of school bands, professional acts, food trucks, games, and community celebration on the future pavilion's doorstep.

MusicFest 2025 filled the greenway with music, families, and a headline set by Skyrocket. It was our proof of concept: the audience is here, the site works, and Southwest Houston shows up for free live music.

What to expect

A preview of pavilion life

Free, always

Admission is free for everyone, in keeping with the Levitt mission. Bring a blanket or a lawn chair and settle in.

All-ages lineup

School and professional bands share the stage across a full day, reflecting the range of Houston's music community.

Food and fun

Food trucks, games, and family activities across one of Houston's most beautiful outdoor spaces.

Levitt Pavilion Houston

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston is a Texas nonprofit bringing free, live music to Southwest Houston at the future Levitt Pavilion beside Willow Waterhole Greenway.
