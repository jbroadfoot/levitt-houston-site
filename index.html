Coming to Southwest Houston

Free live music
under the stars

Levitt Pavilion Houston will create a permanent home for free live music beside the 291-acre Willow Waterhole Greenway. Each year, the pavilion will welcome Houston to 40 to 50 free professional concerts in an open-air setting built for music, connection, and community.

The Place

Where Houston's next great gathering place begins

Levitt Pavilion Houston is planned for approximately 8 acres of the former Shell Gasmer site beside Willow Waterhole Greenway. The historic steel derrick will remain as a landmark connecting Houston’s energy heritage to a new gathering place for music and community.

For more than 25 years, neighbors have transformed Willow Waterhole into 291 acres of lakes, trails, and prairie. The pavilion will add a welcoming place to gather at the Greenway’s future front door.

Already making music

Houston shows up for free live music

MusicFest at Willow Waterhole. Photo: © Eduardo Perme.

MusicFest has brought free live music to Willow Waterhole since 2013. The Spring 2026 event continued that tradition, bringing student musicians, professional artists, families, and neighbors together around the stage. Levitt Houston will build on that community response through future concerts and MusicFest events.
