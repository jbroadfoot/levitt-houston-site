The Levitt Network

A proven model,
coast to coast

From a former town dump in Westport to the blast furnaces of Bethlehem, Levitt venues turn overlooked places into beloved ones.

The mission

Building community through music

For five decades the Levitt Foundation has turned underused public spaces into thriving destinations with one idea: free, live music brings people together. What began on a former town dump in Westport, Connecticut is now a national movement.

Houston's place

Joining a national family of venues

Levitt Pavilion Houston is one of only two Levitt venues in development in America. Every Levitt venue pairs the Foundation's investment, standards, and national network with a local nonprofit that programs the venue and produces the concerts. Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston fills that role here.

The venues

A network from coast to coast

Permanent Levitt venues anchor communities across the country, each free, each open lawn, each shaped by its own city.

Westport, CT

Where it began in 1974, on a reclaimed former town dump beside the Saugatuck River.

Pasadena, CA

Free concerts in the heart of Memorial Park.

Los Angeles, CA

A stage for one of the country's most diverse communities in MacArthur Park.

Memphis, TN

Free music at the historic Overton Park Shell, where generations of Memphis music history live on.

Arlington, TX

Downtown Arlington's gathering place, our closest Levitt neighbor in Texas.

Bethlehem, PA

Built beneath the retired blast furnaces of Bethlehem Steel at the SteelStacks arts campus.

Denver, CO

Fifty free concerts a year drawing thousands to Ruby Hill Park.

Dayton, OH

A downtown lawn that brought free live music back to the city's core.

Sioux Falls, SD

Opened at Falls Park in 2019 after a community campaign much like ours.

Levitt Pavilion Houston

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston is the Texas nonprofit building and programming Levitt Pavilion Houston, a free outdoor music venue rising beside Willow Waterhole Greenway in Southwest Houston.
