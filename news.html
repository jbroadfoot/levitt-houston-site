Milestones

Follow the major steps shaping Levitt Pavilion Houston, from its beginnings at Willow Waterhole to the future pavilion planned for the former Shell Gasmer site.

Milestones

The road to opening night

  • 2012 Levitt selects Willow Waterhole. The Levitt Foundation selects Willow Waterhole as the Houston location for a future Levitt venue.
  • 2016 Houston advances the pavilion. Houston City Council approves plans to advance Levitt Pavilion Houston.
  • 2019 The future location shifts to Gasmer. The City of Houston acquires the former Shell Gasmer property. The future pavilion location later moves to this site beside Willow Waterhole.
  • Spring 2026 MusicFest continues the tradition. MusicFest brings student performers, professional artists, families, and neighbors together for free live music at Willow Waterhole Greenway.
  • 2026 The project enters its next phase. Key project agreements move Levitt Houston forward. The Southwest Redevelopment Authority is advancing the purchase of approximately 17 acres from the City of Houston; the transaction has not yet closed.
  • What comes next Planning, fundraising, and future music. Property work, broader site planning, pavilion design, fundraising, and future Levitt programming continue to advance.
