A public-private partnership

Built together, on purpose

In June 2026, three parties signed a Memorandum of Understanding to develop Levitt Pavilion Houston. Each brings something essential: land, capital, national expertise, and local leadership.

Levitt Foundation

The Mortimer and Mimi Levitt Foundation has pledged a $500,000 capital grant, $1.1 million in first-five-years operating support, and at least $200,000 annually through 2041, alongside the standards and support of the national Levitt network.

Southwest Houston Redevelopment Authority / TIRZ 20

The Authority is acquiring the 17-acre Gasmer site, leading its master plan, and leasing roughly 7 acres to Levitt Houston for 30 years at $1 per year, anchoring public investment in the project.

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston

Our Texas nonprofit leads the capital campaign, will produce 40 to 50 free concerts annually, and will operate and maintain the pavilion for the community, year after year.
