The Campaign

A New Kind of Energy for Houston

Levitt Pavilion Houston will transform the former Shell Gasmer site into a permanent home for free live music beside Willow Waterhole Greenway, a place where Houston’s energy history becomes the backdrop for its next great gathering place. Help build the stage.

Be Part of the Beginning

Levitt Pavilion Houston is taking shape through the people who believe in it early. Annual Fund

Keep the Music Playing

MusicFest brings free live music to Willow Waterhole, and it’s the clearest sign of what Levitt Pavilion Houston is becoming: a home for free concerts, open to everyone. Your gift to the Annual Fund supports MusicFest and helps Levitt Houston grow toward that future.
