The Vision

A stage for
Southwest Houston

Levitt Pavilion Houston will create a permanent home for free live music, a welcoming outdoor destination where people from across Houston can gather, connect, and experience music together.

The place

Two transformations, one story

Levitt Pavilion Houston is planned for the former Shell Gasmer property beside Willow Waterhole Greenway.

For more than 25 years, neighbors have helped transform Willow Waterhole into a remarkable landscape of lakes, trails, prairie, and gathering spaces. The former Shell property is now being envisioned as a welcoming new entrance to the Greenway.

Levitt Houston will bring these two stories together, turning land shaped by Houston’s energy history into a new destination for music, nature, and community.

The former Shell Gasmer property beside Willow Waterhole Greenway.

The experience

Music under the stars, open to everyone

Early concept rendering. The pavilion design will continue to develop through the planning process.

The future pavilion will pair a professional outdoor stage with an open lawn designed for relaxed, welcoming concerts. Families and friends will bring blankets and lawn chairs, enjoy food and music, and share an evening together beneath the Houston sky.

Each year, Levitt Houston will present 40 free professional concerts featuring established performers, emerging artists, and a range of musical traditions. Community programs and performances will create additional opportunities for students, local artists, and cultural organizations.

Across the country, Levitt venues have shown how free outdoor music can bring people together and strengthen public spaces. Houston will build on that proven model in a setting unlike any other in the network.

The setting

A place where music and nature meet

Willow Waterhole has already shown how strongly Southwest Houston responds when live music comes to the Greenway.

MusicFest at Willow Waterhole, where live music, open space, and community already come together. Photo: © 2018 ev1pro.com + EAMD.

MusicFest has brought students, professional artists, families, and neighbors together here since 2013. Levitt Houston will give that community experience a permanent home, with the stage, lawn, production facilities, and support needed to present free concerts year after year.

The landmark

A visible connection to the site’s past

The historic steel derrick will remain as a distinctive landmark within the former Shell property. It will preserve a visible link to Houston’s energy heritage while marking a new chapter centered on music, public space, and community life.

The impact
