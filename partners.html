A public-private partnership

Built together, on purpose

Levitt Pavilion Houston is built on a partnership of national philanthropy, public investment, and community leadership. Each partner brings something essential.

Levitt Foundation

The national foundation behind the Levitt network invests in every venue it partners with and brings the standards, experience, and support of more than 100 Levitt communities.

Southwest Houston Redevelopment Authority

The public partner leading planning for the former Shell Gasmer site and anchoring public investment in the project.

Friends of Levitt Pavilion Houston

The Texas nonprofit leading the effort to build, program, and sustain the pavilion, presenting 40 to 50 free concerts a year for the community.
